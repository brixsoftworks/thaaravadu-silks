Our Heritage

Three Generations of Weaving Excellence

For over seven decades, Thaaravadu Silks has been synonymous with the finest Balaramapuram handloom. Our family tradition is rooted in the meticulous art of weaving, employing natural dyes and authentic golden zari to create masterpieces that stand the test of time.

Each saree is a labor of love, requiring 2 to 3 weeks of dedicated craftsmanship on traditional wooden looms. We preserve the age-old techniques passed down through generations, ensuring every drape tells a story of Kerala's rich cultural tapestry.
